📌 Project Overview

The PhonePe Payment Analytics Dashboard was developed to monitor digital payment performance, transaction trends, user engagement, and service-wise transaction value. The dashboard provides a centralized view of key business metrics, enabling stakeholders to track payment success rates, analyze customer behavior, and optimize digital payment services.

🎯 Business Problem

As digital payment transactions continue to grow, businesses require a centralized analytics solution to answer critical questions:

How many transactions are processed each month?
What is the total transaction value?
What is the payment success rate?
Which payment services generate the highest transaction value?
Which customer age group contributes the most transactions?
How do weekday and weekend transactions compare?
Who are the highest-value users?

Without an interactive dashboard, monitoring these KPIs and identifying trends was time-consuming and inefficient.

🔍 Key Insights
300K transactions generated a total transaction value of ₹3.47 Billion.
The overall payment success rate reached 96%, indicating reliable transaction processing.
Gen X and Millennials contributed nearly 75% of total users, making them the most active customer segments.
Weekday transactions accounted for approximately 72% of total payment activity.
Money Transfer services generated the highest transaction value among all service categories.
Transaction volume remained stable throughout the year, with noticeable peaks during mid-year months.
A small group of high-value users contributed significantly to the overall transaction value.
